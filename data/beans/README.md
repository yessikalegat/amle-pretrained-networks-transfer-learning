# Bean Leaf Disease Images

1,295 photos of bean leaves in three classes (`healthy`, `angular_leaf_spot`, `bean_rust`), in the original split into `train/`, `validation/` and `test/`. The photos were taken with smartphones in bean fields in Uganda.

Source: Makerere AI Lab, [AI-Lab-Makerere/ibean](https://github.com/AI-Lab-Makerere/ibean), MIT licence (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The images in this folder were resized from 500 × 500 to 160 × 160 pixels.
