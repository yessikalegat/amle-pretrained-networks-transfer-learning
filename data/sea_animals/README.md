# Sea Animal Images

335 photos of five categories of [Caltech-101](https://data.caltech.edu/records/mzrjq-6wc02): `crab`, `crayfish`, `dolphin`, `lobster` and `starfish`, split per category into 60 % training (`train/`), 20 % validation (`validation/`) and 20 % test (`test/`) photos with `random_state=42`.

Source: Fei-Fei, L., Fergus, R. and Perona, P. (2004). Learning generative visual models from few training examples. Caltech-101, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The photos are unchanged.
